scenarios under `concord/corpus/` cover EARS ids minted in `doc/spec/`;
`concord/schema/*.md` are the authoring contracts; `concord/corpus/DISPUTES.md`

never hand-edited.

fixes merged. Both entries carry their own resolution clause; this ticket

## Problem

1. **The driver still binds the torn combine.**
   `KernelCatalog.build`'s `combine-latest` branch
   (`concord/src/main/kotlin/civictech/concord/driver/kernel/KernelCatalog.kt:133-141`)
   binds `fn: sum` to `ScalarSumCombineCell` and throws for anything else. The
   new kernel coalescing combine is bound nowhere, so no scenario can drive the
   scalar combine glitch-free.
2. **`24-OP-COMBINE-01` under-asserts.** It covers only `[21-PROP-01]`
   (`24-OP-COMBINE-01.yaml:13`) with a `final-view` golden; its header comment
   documents the omitted stream-level check. Note also that its current graph —
   two **independent** counter sources (`:20-29`) — contains no fork-join, so
   every wave is single-arm and a glitch-free assertion over that graph would
   be **vacuous**. The shape that genuinely tears is the diamond: one
   `counter-source` forked through two identity arms into the summing combine —
   exactly `CTL-GF-01.yaml:17-29`.
3. **`CTL-GF-01` is about to invert.** A `kind: control` scenario MUST fail;
   the runner asserts its failure
   (`concord/src/test/kotlin/civictech/concord/runner/CorpusRunner.kt:124-129`,
   P7). `CTL-GF-01` is not a deliberately-wrong-golden control like
   `CTL-GOLDEN-01`/`CTL-CONVERGE-01` — it asserts the **true** invariant
   against a known-broken binding. If item 1 makes the binding correct, the
   control starts passing and the runner fails it for *not* failing. It cannot
   simply be left alone.
4. **`24-REPLAY-01` has no scenario, and the dur driver cannot yet spawn its
   graph.** `KernelDriverDur.build`
   (`concord/src/main/kotlin/civictech/concord/driver/kernel/KernelDriverDur.kt:178-193`)
   binds only `set-source`/`journal-set-source`, `set-view`/`journal-set-view`,
   and `effect-sink` on the reserved `dur` host; there is no `quorum-set` arm.
   Its `wire()` (`:213-217`) also casts the link source to `SetApi`, which a
   `quorum-set` (as the upstream of the view link) is not.
5. **The ledger and the generated concordance are stale the moment the above
   lands.** `DISPUTES.md`'s two entries and its category index still file the
   gaps; `doc/spec/CONCORDANCE.md:75` lists `CTL-GF-01` as a coverer of
   `22-GF-01`.
