Model A's POSITIONS shares (IssueNode -> Softmax.shares, demo/deliberate/src/main/kotlin/civictech/deliberate/Semantics.kt `object Softmax`, Cells.kt `IssueNode`; SPEC FRA-03) misstate the distribution in three ways, found by the computenet-1ow0x combination simulation (2026-09-28):

1. Over-sharpening / K=2 inconsistency. share = normalised odds of each position's credence (T=1). For two complementary positions (credences p and 1-p) that is softmax(l, -l) = sigmoid(2·logit p): a 0.7 verdict shows as 0.84. Only T=2 matches the binary verdict in that case. Checked in the sim against the woe layer: max diff 0.150 at T=1, 0.000 at T=2.
2. No "none of these". When every position is implausible (e.g. each credence 0.1), or every position has been argued down, shares are still 1/n each — the UI shows a confident-looking even split instead of "none of the listed answers holds".
3. Prior sharpening. With no arguments, first-impression plausibilities (0.15, 0.2, 0.7) show as shares (0.064, 0.091, 0.845), where simple normalisation gives (0.14, 0.19, 0.67). The hero distribution overstates the leader before any deliberation.

Scope: 1 is a one-constant question (T) but T=2 is only principled for complementary pairs; 2 needs an Ω/"none" share (plausibility residual) or at least a displayed absolute signal (e.g. max credence / residual) beside the shares. The proper multi-class treatment is being designed in computenet-1ow0x (log-linear elimination + Ω class); this bug is the interim honesty fix for the shipped model A, and may be superseded by it — decide which before implementing.
