# Graphalytics validation fixtures

These are the two smallest published validation datasets from
`github.com/ldbc/ldbc_graphalytics`, checked out at commit
`7b8bde76cf7aab5e90b25ecd4b38829e2f98b292`.

The dataset and expected-output files come from
`graphalytics-validation/src/main/resources/validation-graphs/example/`:

- `example-directed/example-directed.v`
- `example-directed/example-directed.e`
- `example-directed/example-directed-BFS`
- `example-directed/example-directed-CDLP`
- `example-directed/example-directed-LCC`
- `example-directed/example-directed-PR`
- `example-directed/example-directed-SSSP`
- `example-directed/example-directed-WCC`
- `example-undirected/example-undirected.v`
- `example-undirected/example-undirected.e`
- `example-undirected/example-undirected-BFS`
- `example-undirected/example-undirected-CDLP`
- `example-undirected/example-undirected-LCC`
- `example-undirected/example-undirected-PR`
- `example-undirected/example-undirected-SSSP`
- `example-undirected/example-undirected-WCC`

The properties files come from `config-template/graphs/`:

- `example-directed.properties`
- `example-undirected.properties`

The checked-in `LICENSE` is the upstream repository's Apache-2.0 license text.
